Doors and windows are only deducted when they are large (patio doors, bay windows). For standard sizes, you'll still need full strips above and below each opening — so the calculator applies a partial credit only, as professional decorators recommend.
Look at the label on your wallpaper roll or the product page online. You'll see a symbol that looks like two overlapping squares, followed by a measurement in inches or cm. That number is your pattern repeat. If you see "0" or a dash, your wallpaper has no repeat — select "No Pattern" above instead.
